Queen Ranavalona I of Madagascar

Ranavalomanjaka - The Sovereign Queen
Commemorating Her Reign · 1828-1861
I take what serves this land; Scorn my ancestors, and I will strike it down.
- Queen Ranavalona I
The Guiding Principle of Sovereignty
"Mahasoa, mandrosoa. Mahamono, mòdia"
Mahasoa
Beneficial: Foreign wisdom, knowledge, and skillset that can strengthen the nation
Mandrosoa
Welcome: Let it enter and advance within our nation for progress
Mahamono
Destructive: Anything harmful or incompatible with our sovereign nation's values
Mòdia
Expel: Send it home, reject it from our land to preserve our sovereignty
Queen Ranavalona I's foundational principle for Madagascar: Selectively embrace beneficial foreign knowledge while protecting cultural sovereignty

Historical Significance

Ranavalona I (c. 1788 - August 16, 1861) reigned as Queen of Madagascar for 33 years, fiercely defending Malagasy sovereignty against European colonialism. Her 1835 proclamation stands as a landmark declaration of cultural preservation and selective modernization.

"The wisdom of 'Mahasoa, mandrosoa. Mahamono, mòdia' became the cornerstone of Ranavalona's reign. This philosophy allowed Madagascar to modernize its military and infrastructure while maintaining its cultural independence during the height of European colonial expansion."

The 1835 Proclamation

Date: Antananarivo, 26 February 1835
Recipients: "All foreigners, whether English or French"

Original Malagasy
English Translation
French Translation
Any ny vazaha rehetra na Anglisy na frantsay, Ary lazaiko aminareo havako, fa noho ny soa nataonareo tamin'ny taniko sy ny fanjakako, nampianatra fahendrena sy fahalalana, dia misaotra anareo aho amin'izany, fa mamiko sy teliko izany. Hitako ny nataonareo tamin-dRadama, ary nony mba amiko tsy manova hianareo, fa manohy izay hahatsara ity tany ity. Ary lazaiko aminareo vazaha rehetra koa fa raha mbola mitoetra eto amin'ny taniko hianareo dia mahazo manao izay fomban'drazanareo sy fanaonareo rehetra. Aza manambadipo, fa tsy manova izay fomban-drazanareo sy fanaonareo aho, fa tsara ny nataonareo tamin'ny taniko sy ny fankakako; nefa kosa na ataoko aza izany, raha ny lalan'ny taniko no dakaina, matiko izay manao izany, na zovy na zovy, nefa tsy eto ihany no tahaka izany, fa na aiza na aiza, maty izay mandika ny lalan'ny tany. Ary tsy izany ihany, fa lazaiko marimarina aminareo koa, fa raha izao vahoakako izao manova ny fomban-drazana, izay fanao hatramin'ny Roambinifolo Manjaka ka hatramin'Andrianampoinimerina sy Ilehidama, raha izany no ovana, laviko izany, fa izay napetraky ny razako tsy mba mahamenatra ahy na mahatahotra ahy. Izay fahedrena sy fahalalana tokony hahasoa ity tany ity, ekeko izany; fa raha ny fomban-drazako kosa no kasikasihina, sasara ny olon-ko hariva ny andro, fa laviko izany. Koa indro ny amin'ny fivavahana, na Alahady na ankobabandro, ny badisa, ny sosaiety, tsy azon'ny vahoakako atao eto amin'ny taniko izany; ary hianareo vazaha kosa, raha mbola misy taozavatra sy fahedrena tokony hahasoa ny ambanilanitra tianareo hampianarina, ataovy fa tsara izany. Izany ary no teniko, ka tiako ho renareo havako.
To all foreigners, whether English or French, I say to you, my relatives, that because of the good you have done to my land and my kingdom, teaching wisdom and knowledge, I thank you for that, for I understand and know that. I have seen what you did during Radama's time, and if you do the same with me without changing, but continue what will improve this land. And I say to all foreigners too that if you still reside here in my land you may practice all your ancestral customs and traditions. Do not change, because I do not change your ancestral customs and practices, for good is what you have done to my land and my kingdom; but if that is done, if the law of my land is violated, I will punish whoever does that, whoever it may be, and not only here, but anywhere, punishment to whoever breaks the law of the land. And not only that, but I declare clearly to you also, that if my people change the ancestral customs, the practices from the time of the Twelve Sacred Kings until Andrianampoinimerina and Radama, if that is changed, I will eradicate it, for what was established by my ancestors does not shame me nor frighten me. The wisdom and knowledge that should benefit this land, I accept that; but if my ancestral customs are scorned, even if the sun sets at noon, I will eradicate it. Therefore, concerning religion, whether Sunday or any day, baptism, societies, my people are not allowed to practice that here in my land; and you foreigners, if there are still crafts and wisdom that will benefit the nation that you wish to teach, do so, for that is good. That then is my word, and I wish that you hear it, my relatives.
À tous les étrangers, qu'ils soient Anglais ou Français, Je vous dis à vous, la parenté, qu'en raison du bien que vous avez fait à ma terre et à mon royaume, en enseignant la sagesse et la connaissance, je vous en remercie, car je le comprends et le sais. J'ai vu ce que vous avez fait à l'époque de Radama, et si vous faites de même avec moi sans changer, mais continuez ce qui améliorera cette terre. Et je dis aussi à tous les étrangers que si vous résidez encore ici dans ma terre, vous pouvez pratiquer toutes vos coutumes ancestrales et traditions. Ne changez pas, car je ne change pas vos coutumes ancestrales et pratiques, car le bien est ce que vous avez fait pour ma terre et mon royaume ; mais si cela est fait, si la loi de ma terre est violée, je punirai quiconque fait cela, quels qu'il soit, et pas seulement ici, mais partout, punition pour quiconque enfreint la loi du pays. Et non seulement cela, mais je déclare clairement à vous aussi, que si mon peuple change les coutumes ancestrales, les pratiques depuis l'époque des Douze Rois Sacrés jusqu'à Andrianampoinimerina et Radama, si cela est changé, je l'éradiquerai, car ce qui a été établi par mes ancêtres ne me fait pas honte ni ne m'effraie. La sagesse et la connaissance qui devraient profiter à ce pays, je l'accepte ; mais si mes coutumes ancestrales sont méprisées, même si le soleil se couche à midi, je l'éradiquerai. C'est pourquoi, en ce qui concerne la religion, que ce soit le dimanche ou n'importe quel jour, le baptême, les sociétés, mon peuple n'a pas le droit de pratiquer cela ici dans ma terre ; et vous, étrangers, s'il y a encore des métiers et une sagesse qui profiteront à la nation que vous souhaitez enseigner, faites-le, car cela est bon. Voilà donc ma parole, et je souhaite que vous l'entendiez, la parenté.
Hoy Ranavalomanjaka
(Thus says the Reigning Ranavalona)
Important Notes:
  • The phrase "matiko izay manao izany" has been properly translated as "I will punish whoever does that" to reflect its true meaning in Malagasy when admonishing someone.
  • The French translation uses "la parenté" (kinship) instead of "mes parents" to better reflect the Malagasy concept of "havako" (my relatives).

Textual Notes

  • Ilehidama - Alternate spelling of Radama (her predecessor)
  • fankakako - Archaic form of fanjakako (my kingdom)
  • olon-ko - oloko (my people)
  • ankobabandro - ankabe andro (any day)
  • Roambinifolo Manjaka - The Twelve Sacred Kings (Merina dynasty founders)
  • matiko izay manao izany - Admonishing meaning: "I will punish whoever does that"
  • Mahasoa, mandrosoa. Mahamono, mòdia - The foundational principle of Ranavalona's sovereignty policy

Historical Legacy

Queen Ranavalona I passed away on August 16, 1861, after a reign that established foundational principles of sovereignty and cultural preservation:

  • Preserved Madagascar's independence during Europe's colonial expansion
  • Modernized the military while rejecting cultural colonization
  • Established the principle of selective technological adoption
  • Maintained Malagasy cultural traditions against foreign influence
  • Created a blueprint for anti-colonial resistance movements
  • Demonstrated female leadership in a male-dominated era
  • Developed the enduring philosophy of "Mahasoa, mandrosoa. Mahamono, mòdia"

Her iconic declarations remain powerful statements of cultural sovereignty studied worldwide.
